01WHO WE ARE

Culture, commerce
& confidence.

BelongingVisibilityJoy

Hoyga Hodan (“Hodan’s Hub”) is a grassroots community-based organization founded in 2025 and dedicated to empowering youth women in Somalia through creative entrepreneurship, skills training, and community-driven economic development.

Founded and led by Somali women, we operate at the intersection of culture, commerce, and confidence-building — creating safe, affordable spaces where young women learn, connect, and launch businesses.

We believe that economic empowerment is not just about skills — it is about belonging, visibility, and joy. Every Hoyga Hodan event combines rigorous training with cultural celebration, mentorship with market access, and individual growth with community solidarity.

Our goal is to create a sustainable ecosystem where youth women and businesses thrive together, strengthening the fabric of the Mogadishu community and beyond.

— Hoyga Hodan Founding Vision

02OUR STORY

From one $5 ticket to a movement.

Hoyga Hodan began as a simple idea: what if Somali youth women had a space that was theirs — affordable, creative, and built for their realities?

2025 · THE IDEA

A space that was theirs

Founder Hodan Abdullaahi Mohamed launched the first Hoyga Hodan event in Mogadishu with community trust, local partnerships, and a $5 ticket model.

EVENT 01 · 150 WOMEN

They left with confidence

That first event brought together 150 young women. They painted calabashes, shared business ideas, danced Baraanbur, and left with something rare: confidence.

THE SPARK

Word spread. Media followed.

Social media exploded. Local TV covered it. The demand for this kind of space was not just real — it was urgent.

EDITIONS 02–04

Each larger, each deeper

Three more editions followed — including the Baraawe chapter launch and the Dhaqan Goob cultural corner — each proving the model.

TODAY · 2026

Ready to scale

We have proven the model. We have the community. We have the media partnership. What we need now is institutional backing to take this national.

03WHAT WE DO

Four engines of empowerment.

01

Skills Training & Workshops

The backbone of Hoyga Hodan is hands-on, market-relevant training delivered by local experts. Our half-day workshops cover:

Business PlanningAI & Digital InnovationContent Creation Financial LiteracyLeadership & Public SpeakingCreative Crafts
02

Hoyga Hodan Events

Our signature multi-day events combine training with celebration. Each event includes:

  • Inspirational talks by successful women entrepreneurs
  • Success story sessions where participants share journeys
  • Business exhibitions with vendor stalls
  • Panel discussions on women’s economic participation
  • Networking with mentors and buyers
  • Creative & cultural performances — Baraanbur, Dhaanto, poetry
  • Awards & recognition for outstanding participants
  • Media interviews and photography coverage
03

Mentorship & Market Linkages

Training without follow-up is wasted. We match participants with local business leaders for ongoing mentorship, and we organize quarterly market expos that connect entrepreneurs directly with buyers, investors, and government procurement officers.

Mentor MatchingQuarterly Market ExposBuyers & InvestorsProcurement Access
04

Digital Platform

We are building an online learning portal to reach youth women in rural areas and the diaspora — extending the Hub far beyond Mogadishu.

Somali-Language Training VideosMentor Matching SystemBusiness DirectoryDigital Certificates
