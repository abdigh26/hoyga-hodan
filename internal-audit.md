# Hoyga Hodan Website Audit

## Scope

This audit compares the existing static site implementation with the supplied Hoyga Hodan content brief. The update should evolve the current editorial site rather than rebuild it. The source of truth for corrected figures and planned activities is the supplied brief; the existing homepage is treated as the design and implementation baseline.

## KEEP

The existing visual identity should remain: cream and sand surfaces, crimson and maroon accents, orange and amber highlights, Fraunces and Outfit typography, editorial collage, cloth-texture bands, animated reveals, photographic storytelling, programme cards, impact panels, responsive navigation, gallery masonry, lightbox controls, looping films, and the current single-page architecture.

The existing asset library should remain in use. It provides photographs and videos that make the organization feel human and culturally specific. Existing descriptive alt text should be retained where accurate and tightened where the image caption or event context changes.

The current sections are structurally valuable: the hero, Who We Are, Our Story, What We Do, Track Record, Gallery & Films, Team, Partners, Roadmap, Theory of Change, Why Partner With Us, Alignment, and Contact. They provide a strong narrative arc and can be reordered or strengthened without introducing a generic NGO template.

The responsive navigation, scrollspy, reveal animations, counters, image lightbox, video pause controls, reduced-motion handling, and contact form UX should be preserved. The existing `admin.html` messages viewer should remain, but its API access and security limitations need correction.

## CHANGE

### Hero and positioning

Replace the current primary positioning around “Youth Women Empowerment Through Creative Entrepreneurship” and the old $5 ticket language with the clearer participant-centered idea: “A space where Somali women learn, connect, create and thrive.” The supporting copy should explain that Hoyga Hodan combines practical skills and entrepreneurship training with community activities, cultural experiences, networking, mentorship, creativity, and joy.

Change the primary CTA to “Join an Event” and add a clear secondary “Support Hoyga Hodan” pathway. The page should make a potential participant feel invited while giving partners and donors a credible route into the organization.

### Participant contribution and historical impact

Correct every $5 reference. The supplied brief states that the participant contribution is $10 and that the existing $5 references are a typo. Do not create a price-change narrative.

Replace unsupported or overbroad impact claims with the supplied reconstructed figures and careful approximate language: 4+ events delivered, approximately 600 women reached, 422 paid participants, and 178 free participants. These are historical figures and should not be presented as audited.

### Event history

Add a clearly labeled historical-events view or timeline with the four events and approximate participation details: Eco Hub — Mogadishu, approximately 100 participants with 59 paid and 41 free; Muniiza Hall — Mogadishu, approximately 150 participants with 92 paid and 58 free; Dhaqan Gooob — Mogadishu, 200 participants with 140 paid and 60 free; and Baraawe — South West State / Lower Shabelle, 150 participants with 131 paid and 19 free.

The existing “Baraawe Chapter” framing should be revised so Baraawe is presented as an important participant-demand story without inventing direct quotes or testimonials. Add a story structure that can later receive an authentic participant name, city, portrait, quote, video, and story after those materials are approved.

### Planned expansion

Separate past results from planned expansion. Add the 12-month plan as a clearly marked plan: 9 planned events across 4 planned cities—Mogadishu (5), Baidoa (1), Garoowe (2), and Hargeisa (1). Describe the expected size as 100–150 women per event and approximately 1,125 planned participant places, not guaranteed beneficiaries or delivered impact.

Remove or soften claims that imply the organization has already delivered events in cities that are only planned. The existing 2026–2031 national roadmap can remain as a vision, but it must be labeled as a forward-looking aspiration rather than a current footprint.

### Programme model

Make the distinction explicit: Hoyga Hodan is not simply a training center. Add a memorable “More Than a Training” section with six participant experiences: Learn, Connect, Create, Play, Celebrate, and Continue. Frame games, competitions, art, music, karaoke, and social activities as structured community and cultural activities that encourage confidence, participation, connection, and belonging—not as entertainment appended to training.

Add a visual programme journey: Learn → Connect → Play → Create → Build Confidence → Build Community → Access Opportunity.

### Funding and support pathway

Replace any claim that the $5 ticket model proves financial sustainability. The correct message is that the first four events were made possible through $10 participant contributions, local businesses, donated services, free food and water, volunteer trainers and staff, discounted or free media, occasional financial support, and founder/team effort. This demonstrated community demand but is difficult to sustain because support is unpredictable.

Use the accurate fundraising language: Hoyga Hodan is building a more predictable and sustainable funding model and is seeking institutional and strategic partners for its next stage. Do not publish the $56,000 figure or invent a final annual target.

Create four support pathways: Fund a Programme, Sponsor a Participant, Become a Partner, and Support Expansion. Mention possible partners—UN agencies, foundations, NGOs, development organizations, businesses, and local partners—without implying that any such partnership already exists. Mention possible support types: programme funding, participant sponsorship, venues, food and water, trainers, media, materials, mentorship, and market connections.

### Team

Replace the current invented or unverified staff biographies with the supplied current team structure: one unpaid Founder/Director, one unpaid Program/Operations team member, one unpaid Finance team member, one project-based paid Developer, one paid Media team member, two currently unpaid Trainers, and three to five unpaid Volunteers. Future institutional support may strengthen programme management, finance/admin, monitoring and evaluation, and communications, but these should not be presented as current established positions.

### Impact and M&E

Retain a proof-oriented impact section, but distinguish reconstructed historical numbers from future monitoring. Add a light “What we will measure next” structure for attendance, completion, participant satisfaction, skills gained, networking connections, mentorship, business/action plans, and businesses started or improved. Use “to be measured” or equivalent placeholders instead of invented results.

### Navigation and conversion

Keep the one-page architecture but simplify the visible navigation around Home, About, Programmes, Events, Stories, Impact, Partners, and Contact. Add a persistent or prominent “Join an Event” CTA and a clear “Support” CTA. Existing section anchors can support this without adding unnecessary pages.

## REMOVE OR REWRITE

Remove or rewrite all visible $5 ticket references, including the hero ticket element, story language, impact language, and partner copy. Replace with the correct $10 participant contribution only where the contribution is contextually useful.

Remove claims that the ticket model proves financial sustainability. Replace with a careful description of community demand and an emerging funding model.

Remove or soften “600+ trained” where the supplied figure is more accurately approximately 600 women reached/participants across four events. Avoid presenting the number as an audited training completion count.

Remove unsupported numerical claims such as the existing “90% ticket sales rate,” “20+ local businesses exhibited,” “≈$30 per participant trained,” “800 trained annually,” “5,000 cumulative,” and “15,000 cumulative alumni” unless the user later provides documentation that supports them. These figures should not remain as established facts in the donor-facing site.

Remove or rewrite “registration in progress” unless the user confirms the organization’s current legal-registration status. Avoid publishing any legal-status claim without confirmation.

Remove fabricated or over-specific team biographies. Do not present the existing four named roles as current verified positions unless the user confirms them.

Remove any direct participant quote that is not supplied or approved. Baraawe should be described through verified observations of participant happiness, connection, and requests for more gatherings, not fabricated quotation marks.

## ADD

Add a participant-centered hero with the two CTAs “Join an Event” and “Support Hoyga Hodan.”

Add the “More Than a Training” six-part experience section and the visual programme journey.

Add a factual historical-events module and a separate planned-expansion module.

Add a Baraawe participant-story placeholder designed for future approved portrait, quote, video, city, and story content.

Add a four-pathway support module without a fixed fundraising target.

Add a transparent historical funding-model explanation and a future M&E indicator framework.

Improve SEO metadata, Open Graph metadata, canonical URL, semantic heading hierarchy, skip link, focus states, button labels, image lazy loading, and reduced-motion behavior. Add `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` where appropriate for the public domain.

Fix the contact API method gate so the GET messages endpoint used by `admin.html` actually works. Review the storage approach because a JSON file written in a serverless deployment is not durable across instances; preserve the current interface but document the limitation and avoid promising permanent message storage until durable storage or an email provider is configured.

## Factual cautions

The supplied brief is the controlling source for the corrected figures and plan. The site should use “approximately,” “planned,” “expected,” and “to be measured” where required. It should not invent donors, UN partnerships, government partnerships, grants, awards, testimonials, participant quotes, outcomes, registrations, cities already reached, funding received, or a final annual budget.

## Implementation priorities

1. Correct factual claims and CTA language.
2. Add the participant experience and support pathways.
3. Separate historical results from planned expansion.
4. Preserve gallery, lightbox, video, navigation, animations, assets, and single-page architecture.
5. Fix the contact API/admin viewer path and improve SEO/accessibility.
6. Test desktop and mobile behavior before publishing.
7. Ask the user to approve any remaining unverified names, dates, links, or contact details before they are treated as final public facts.
